# Toyota Land Cruiser 300 Audio Guide

This guide is aimed at a 2023 Toyota Land Cruiser 300 GXL in Australia and is built around three strict rules:

1. No drilling, no splicing, or permanent modification.
2. 100% Plug-and-play DIY integration.
3. Smooth, low-fatigue sound for long listening sessions.

The goal is not loudness. The goal is clean headroom, controlled bass, and a tonal balance that does not become tiring after an hour on the road.

## Fact-check scope and confidence

This page was updated on 2026-08-11 to reflect strict DIY realities:

- **No-Drill Enforcement:** Front speakers must be 2-way to utilize factory locations. 3-way setups require custom A-pillar fabrication and have been rejected.
- **No-Splicing Enforcement:** Amplifiers must feature an explicit Toyota T-Harness ecosystem.
- **Timbre Matching:** The front and rear stage share a uniform speaker brand to maintain a consistent acoustic character across the cabin.

## DIY Shopping List

Use this as your exact purchase list. These parts are selected specifically because they do not require woodworking, fiberglassing, or wire cutting.

### DSP / Amplifier
- **Focal Impulse 4.320** (with Focal Toyota T-Harness)

## 1. Amplifier options

For a strict DIY use case, the best amplifier is the one that plugs directly into the factory harness without cutting a single wire.

### Primary Candidates
- **Focal Impulse 4.320:** A highly compact amplifier that uses a dedicated Focal Toyota Y-cable (T-Harness). It fits easily under a seat and intercepts the factory signal flawlessly.
- **Beat-Sonic Encore Alpha:** Specifically engineered for Toyota standard head units. It provides a direct plug-and-play harness to boost power without splicing.

### Mounting
Do not drill into the floorpan. Use industrial-strength hook-and-loop (Velcro) to secure the amplifier directly to the carpet under the passenger seat.

## 2. Front speakers

To maintain the strict "No-Drill" rule, we must use a **2-way component system**. Upgrading to a 3-way system would require destructive custom molding on the A-pillars.

### Morel Maximo Ultra 602 MKII
This is the top candidate for a smooth, studio-like presentation that avoids high-frequency harshness (fatigue). 

### Installation Crucials
- **Adapter Rings:** You must use the **Aerpro APS299** plastic adapter rings. These bolt directly into the factory holes, allowing the 6.5" Morel woofer to drop in perfectly.
- **Tweeter Mounting:** The factory dash tweeter location is tight. You will use the included Morel brackets, though minor trimming of the hidden plastic structure *under* the factory grille may be required to snap the grille back down.

## 3. Rear door speakers

Rear speakers should be supportive, not attention-grabbing. 

### Timbre Matching
By using the **Morel Maximo Ultra Coaxials** in the rear, you achieve "timbre matching." This means the sonic signature of the system remains perfectly uniform. When a sound pans from front to rear, its character does not change. Keep the rear amplifier gain conservative so it pulls the soundstage backward only slightly, enriching the cabin feel.

## 4. Subwoofer

The GXL model has an empty plastic panel in the cargo area where higher trims feature a factory subwoofer. **Do not attempt to use this space for DIY.** Hiding a subwoofer there requires a highly expensive custom fiberglass "stealthbox" and often requires drilling into the metal body to secure it.

### The No-Drill DIY Solution
Use the **JL Audio ACP110LG-TW1**. It is a fantastic powered enclosure that provides deep, controlled bass without needing a separate amplifier.

To secure it without drilling:
1. Place the enclosure in the rear cargo area.
2. Route heavy-duty cam straps through the vehicle's factory metal D-rings (tie-down points).
3. Strap the enclosure tightly. It remains safe during transit and is easily removable if you need extra boot space.

## 5. Sound dampening

Good damping makes the system sound calmer at normal volume and improves midbass punch.

### Where to focus
1. **Front doors:** Focus here first. Apply CLD tiles to the outer skin (inside the door cavity) and the inner metal skin.
2. **Adapter rings:** Add a thin layer of closed-cell foam between the Aerpro adapter ring and the metal door to prevent plastic-on-metal buzzing.
3. **Rear doors:** Apply moderate damping to match the front.

Do not overbuild. CLD should stop panel resonance, not blanket every surface.

## 6. Suggested build order

If you want the cleanest path, do it in this order:

1. **Pre-wire:** Install the Focal or Beat-Sonic T-harness behind the radio and route the cables to the passenger seat.
2. **Dampen & Mount Fronts:** Install the Aerpro rings, dampen the front doors, and mount the Morel components.
3. **Rear Fill:** Install the rear Morels.
4. **Subwoofer:** Strap the JL Audio sub in the rear and route its power/signal wire.
5. **Tune:** Keep the rear level conservative and the subwoofer crossover relatively low (~80Hz) to blend seamlessly with the Morels.
